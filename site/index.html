Privacy first

ShieldPal has no account and no servers of its own.

  • Messages, notifications, app lists and Deep Scan results are checked on your phone and never uploaded.
  • Optional online checks send only a small piece (for example a website name) and only when you use them.
  • 2FA secrets, keys and your PIN are encrypted with the Android Keystore.
  • No ads, no analytics, no selling data. “Delete all my data” erases everything in one tap.
