# Security policy

ShieldPal is a security app, so reports about vulnerabilities are taken seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not open a public issue** for a security problem. Email
[usmanfaraz1818@gmail.com](mailto:usmanfaraz1818@gmail.com) with:

- what you found and the steps to reproduce it,
- the app version (Settings → About) and your Android version,
- any proof of concept you are happy to share.

You will get a reply as soon as possible. Please give a reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosing it publicly.

## Scope

- The Android app (Live Guard, Shield VPN, Deep Scan, link / message / QR checks, local storage).
- The website and live demo at <https://usmanfarazz.github.io/shieldpal/>.

## What ShieldPal does *not* do

ShieldPal has no account and no servers of its own, so there is no backend to attack. Optional online checks go to
third-party services (Cloudflare, Google Safe Browsing, urlscan.io, Have I Been Pwned, and the AI services you choose);
vulnerabilities in those services should be reported to them.
